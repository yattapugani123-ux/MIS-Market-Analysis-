# MIS Market Analysis Dashboard & Insights

## 📊 Project Overview

A data-driven MIS Job Market Analysis project developed during an internship with Analytics Career Connect (ACC).

The project analyzes **50,942 MIS-related job postings** across **11,322 companies, 26 industries, and 600 cities** to understand market demand, hiring patterns, skills, career opportunities, and salary trends.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze MIS job-market demand across India
- Identify leading hiring industries and companies
- Analyze technical and soft-skill requirements
- Identify roles with stronger fresher-level opportunities
- Analyze salary patterns by role, industry, experience, and city
- Provide data-driven placement and career insights
- Build an interactive dashboard for market exploration

## 🔍 Key Insights

- The dataset contains **50,942 MIS job postings** across 11,322 companies, 26 industries, and 600 cities.
- **Banking** is the largest hiring industry in the analyzed dataset, followed by Consulting and Manufacturing.
- **Accenture** is the leading hiring company in the top-company analysis.
- MIS represents the largest department share in the dashboard analysis.
- **Excel** is the most frequently identified technical skill, followed by Reporting, SQL, and Power BI.
- **Communication** is the leading soft skill in the analyzed postings.
- Only **14.9%** of postings are open to freshers, while **85.1%** target experienced candidates.
- The average reported salary is approximately **₹3.98 LPA**, compared with a median of approximately **₹3 LPA**.
